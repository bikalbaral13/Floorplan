1 · The input

Upload the plan-phase deck — PowerPoint (.pptx) or PDF — or one moodboard image (.png .jpg .webp). It is saved in the project's inputs/decks/.

2 · Floorplan and room

The options come from the input: every floorplan it holds, and the rooms on it.

3 · Crops in inputs/images/

The chosen floorplan, its products and swatches, each cropped into its own file in the project's inputs/images/.

4 · Room brief

Claude drafts and curates the room brief from the chosen floorplan; approve it in the step bar.

1 · Overlay, scale and north

Claude puts the chosen floorplan from S1 on the canvas as an overlay. Give one reference to scale it by — a door's width or a printed dimension — and the north bearing.

2 · Walls, doors and windows

Claude traces the walls, doors and windows over the overlay on the canvas. Check them there; say what to improve, or mark them as right.

3 · Objects (library)

Claude places a library object for each piece of furniture and fixture on the plan — real sizes and parameters, matched to the S1 crops. Say what to improve, or mark them as right.

4 · Materials and finishes

Floor, wall paint and fabrics from the S1 swatches, bound to library materials. Say what to change, or mark them as right.

5 · Lighting, new objects and improvements

Claude adds the lights from the moodboard; then anything you want added or changed.

1 · Export the 3D model

The editor turns the final design canvas into 3D and saves it to the project's output/: a GLB and snapshots (isometric, top, front). No Blender, no runner.

2 · Check and approve
