1 · The deck

Upload the plan-phase deck — PowerPoint (.pptx) or PDF — or one moodboard image (.png .jpg .webp): Claude marks its plan, items and swatches. It is saved in the project's inputs/decks/.

S1 · Brief — skill Deck Reader

What the deck cannot settle by itself: answer, save, then Claude drafts and curates the room brief. Approve the brief in the step bar.

S2 · Plan — skill Floorplan Tracer

Claude traces the deck's plan with the Floorplan Tracer (you approve its scale and overlay); the scripts turn the trace into walls, openings and zones. Check geometry_check.png, then approve.

S3 · Objects — skill Object Resolver

Each brief item bound to a library object with its real parameters and a material. Sign off below, then approve.

S4 · Fit-out — skill Floorplan Improver

Every signed-off object placed as given; Claude may add floors, lighting and trims around them. Open the room canvas to check, then approve.

S5 · 3D — skill Floorplan Renderer

The room as .blend / .fbx / .glb with snapshots from the deck's cameras, two corners and the top (Blender on the runner).

Runner

Script stages run on a machine with Python: node tools/runner/worker.mjs in the Floorplan repo. Jobs wait here until it picks them up.
